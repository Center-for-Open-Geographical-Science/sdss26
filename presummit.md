title: "Pre-Summit Briefing"
---

Welcome to the Spatial Data Science Summit.

This meeting is designed to move from vision to structure — strengthening a sustainable, high-impact open-source ecosystem for spatial data science that integrates science, education, government, industry, and software development.

Participants are encouraged to review the themes below and arrive prepared to discuss **strategic priorities, coordination mechanisms, and implementation pathways**.

---

## 🌍 Science

**Objective:** Advance spatial data science as a coherent, reproducible, interdisciplinary scientific enterprise.

### Questions for Reflection

- What major methodological gaps remain in spatial data science?
- Where are aspatial methods still being used when spatial models are warranted?
- What emerging methods in your field should be integrated into the spatial Python stack?
- What infrastructure gaps limit fully reproducible spatial workflows?
- Which disciplines are solving spatial problems in parallel but not interacting?

**Discussion Goal:** Identify 2–3 scientific priorities for coordinated ecosystem development.

---

## 🎓 Education

**Objective:** Build a scalable, modular, globally accessible spatial data science training infrastructure.

### Questions for Reflection

- What core competencies define spatial data science training?
- How should training differ across undergraduate, professional, and doctoral levels?
- What barriers prevent instructors from adopting open-source tools?
- What shared teaching assets would most reduce onboarding costs?
- How can curriculum align directly with workforce and policy needs?
- Where should localization and translation efforts be prioritized?

**Discussion Goal:** Outline a roadmap for a shared, sustainable educational infrastructure.

---

## 🏛 Government & Industry

**Objective:** Strengthen bidirectional integration between spatial data science research and applied decision-making.

### Questions for Reflection

- Where are spatial methods most urgently needed in public decision-making?
- What institutional barriers limit open-source adoption?
- How can open spatial workflows integrate with enterprise infrastructures?
- What spatial capabilities do private firms need but cannot easily access?
- What integration points between open libraries and commercial platforms offer the highest leverage?
- How should real-world use cases systematically inform core library development?

**Discussion Goal:** Define structured engagement mechanisms and advisory pathways.

---

## 🧩 Open Source Development & Governance

**Objective:** Ensure long-term sustainability, scalability, and developer pipeline resilience.

### Questions for Reflection

- What are the largest friction points for new contributors?
- How can domain scientists contribute methods without deep devops expertise?
- What tooling investments would most increase ecosystem coherence?
- What financial model ensures long-term viability?
- How should leadership transition and succession planning be formalized?

**Discussion Goal:** Identify sustainability milestones and governance evolution priorities.

---

## 🔄 Cross-Cutting Strategic Questions

Participants are encouraged to reflect on:

- What would success look like in five years?
- What failure modes are predictable now?
- Where can modest investments generate nonlinear ecosystem returns?
- What coordination problems most constrain ecosystem growth?
- How do we make this ecosystem indispensable rather than optional?

---

## 🎯 Expected Summit Outcomes

By the conclusion of the summit, we aim to identify:

1. **3–5 prioritized ecosystem interventions**
2. A concrete collaboration roadmap (12–24 months)
3. Governance and sustainability action steps
4. Working groups with defined follow-up responsibilities

---

We encourage participants to think in terms of:

- Structure
- Incentives
- Institutional commitments
- Implementation feasibility

This summit is intended to move from ideas to durable infrastructure.
